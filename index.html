From click to decision

Three steps to better decisions.

Analytics is not just collecting numbers. It is a loop: define the question, measure behaviour, then use evidence to improve.

01 / DEFINE

Choose a question

Example: “Where do visitors leave the signup flow?” A clear question helps you choose meaningful events.

02 / MEASURE

Capture the journey

Track relevant actions, compare segments, and check whether your data is complete and trustworthy.

03 / IMPROVE

Test an action

Make one focused change, compare outcomes, and keep it only if the evidence supports it.
